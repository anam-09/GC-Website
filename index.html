Frequently asked questions

How often should I service my AC?

Regular servicing before and during heavy-use seasons keeps cooling efficient. Contact us and we will advise for your unit and usage.

What does an AMC include?

An AMC covers scheduled preventive servicing and breakdown support. Exact terms depend on the plan, so ask us for details.

Do you install ACs bought elsewhere?

Call us with your AC brand and model and we will confirm whether we can help.

Do you serve commercial customers?

Yes. We offer commercial AC solutions and refrigeration services.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Pirangut, Mulshi, Pune. Ask us if your area is covered.
